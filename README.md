In summer of **2022**, I got my first tech job as an **email developer at Covetrus**. It only lasted a couple of months before a September layoff. Fortunately, I had a back-up PT job that I'd kept just in case. 

## Takeda Pharmaceuticals: 2023-2024

My second (and first real) break into tech came in **2023** when I was hired for a role as an **"IT Automation Engineer"** for **Takeda Pharmaceuticals in Exton, Pennsylvania**. Trained as part of the Rising Talent "IT Automation" team, I attended a TEKSystems bootcamp from February through May of 2023, then began work at Takeda in June. During our IT bootcamp, we learned scripting with Python, network automation, Bash, and PowerShell, as well as Git and unit testing. 8 out of 10 of us were advanced to become the official Takeda "Rising Talent IT Automation Team."

**From 2023 through 2024**, our team built tools which accessed data from SharePoint API endpoints. We used our knowledge of automation to improve the workflow for the International Firewall Audit for Cisco and Palo Alto devices. I participated in this international change audit with a small team of three engineers. In addition to the data-related builds and the firewall audit, I acted as the **Pytest/unit test SME** or the **SDET**. I ensured our unit test suites **passed with 100% coverage** and also taught my colleagues about unit testing and the pytest library. Furthermore, the team built a Python-based chatbot with the OpenAI API and Microsoft Azure via API. I wrote the unit tests for this chatbot build.

In **April of 2025**, I began working on Ben Chi's open source codebase, **Dance Chives**. All my work on this codebase is public on Github. 44 commits and a longterm collaborative relationship which later yielded me real paying work from Ben.
